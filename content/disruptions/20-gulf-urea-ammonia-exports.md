<p>Urea is the clearest single case of a chokepoint reaching a farm gate. WTO figures show the price moving from around 400 dollars a tonne to over 850 in April 2026 before falling back to 453 in June, which is the shape of a market that lost a third of its seaborne supply and then found partial substitutes.</p>
