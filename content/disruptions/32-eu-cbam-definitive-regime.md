summary: "The definitive regime of the EU carbon border adjustment mechanism applies from 1 January 2026. EU importers bringing in more than a single mass-based threshold of 50 tonnes a year of covered goods must hold authorised CBAM declarant status, declare the emissions embedded in their imports and surrender certificates against them. Covered sectors are iron and steel, aluminium, cement, fertilisers, hydrogen and electricity. Importers collect verified emissions data through 2026 and the first payments fall in 2027, with a quarterly certificate price for 2026 moving to a weekly price from 1 January 2027."

<p>An omnibus simplification exempts importers below 50 tonnes of covered goods a year, which the Commission describes as excluding about 90 per cent of importers while still capturing about 99 per cent of the emissions. The cash consequence arrives in 2027 even though the data obligation started in January 2026.</p>
