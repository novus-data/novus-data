<p>The stacking rules decide the real number here, not the headline rate. Goods already subject to the semiconductor tariff are exempt from the other Section 232 duties, the Section 301 forced-labour duties of 24 July 2026 do not apply to Section 232 articles, and the Section 122 surcharge expired on 24 July 2026.</p>
