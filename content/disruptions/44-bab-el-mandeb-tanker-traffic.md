<p>The container and tanker trades have decoupled on this route, which is unusual and worth watching. Boxship transits through Suez rose month on month through the autumn while tanker transits at Bab el-Mandeb stayed near their August level, so a single Red Sea recovery narrative does not hold for both.</p>
