<p>Two quota regimes now set the floor under battery metal supply, Kinshasa's for cobalt and Jakarta's for nickel ore, and neither is a market response. S&P Global Commodity Insights is reported to expect nickel to move from surplus into deficit in 2029, which is the horizon on which these decisions bite.</p>
