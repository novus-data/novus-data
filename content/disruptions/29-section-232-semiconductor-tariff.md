<p>Scope is the whole question with this measure: the rate is high but the definition of a covered semiconductor article is narrow, and whether a given part is inside it decides a quarter of its landed cost. Section 232 duties were not affected by the February 2026 Supreme Court ruling that reached the IEEPA tariffs.</p>
