<p>Two regimes are easily confused and the distinction matters for a buyer. The April 2025 licence requirement on seven rare earths and on terbium or dysprosium magnets has never been suspended. What is suspended, until 10 January 2027, is the wider October 2025 package including the rule that would reach products manufactured outside China.</p>
