<p>Aluminium is exposed at Hormuz in both directions: finished metal has to get out and alumina has to get in. Ma'aden, which has its own bauxite, refinery and smelter, kept running and supplied alumina to Alba overland, which is the clearest illustration of why integration mattered more than scale here.</p>
