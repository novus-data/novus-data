<p>This is a forecast rather than a measured shortfall, which is why it is a watch. The useful comparison is the 2023 to 2025 episode, when West African harvest failures took cocoa far above its long-run 2,000 to 3,000 dollar range; the question is whether this warning is the start of a repeat or a correction inside a recovering market.</p>
