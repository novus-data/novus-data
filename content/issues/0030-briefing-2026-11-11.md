The previous suspension ran a year; this one runs two months. China matched it, extending its pause on fees for US-linked ships and its export-control waivers to 10 January. Carriers could have passed those fees on, so the pause lowers that risk through January (inferred). Next: the APEC leaders' meeting in Shenzhen on 18–19 November, where Reuters reports Trump is expected to meet Xi.</p><hr /><img alt="View from the International Space Station of the Strait of Hormuz and the surrounding Gulf coastline" src="https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/4987f94d-0b4b-41d2-9782-fadc21338910/image.jpg?t=1794432683" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><p><i>File photo, 2021: Strait of Hormuz from orbit. Reuters said the 16% fall in the week to 6 November was the steepest since COVID began, and tied the 9 November slide to Trump's 8 November remark that a fresh attack on Iran was highly unlikely while talks continue. JPMorgan estimated an October surplus of about 1.4 million barrels daily. Ships are still being hit: Maritime Executive reported at least two tankers attacked on 7 November, and Stars and Stripes reported US Central Command disabled cargo ship Huda that day, alleging a blockade breach. The deadline had already moved from 1 November to 4 November and then to midnight on 6 November, Bloomberg reported. The contract covers about 2% of the port complex's workers, but the PMA says disruption at the terminals where Local 26 works could affect about 85% of Long Beach cargo and a third of Los Angeles cargo. Bloomberg reported on 5 November that the authority forecast as little as 5 cm by 9 November, against a five-year average above 150 cm, and that Rotterdam–Basel diesel freight was near a record in data back to 2018. Spotbarge's Sebastiaan Kosman told Bloomberg only low-water barges can pass Kaub. Smaller loads mean higher freight per tonne (inferred). Ineos said it has cut Cologne petrochemical output this year because of low water.
