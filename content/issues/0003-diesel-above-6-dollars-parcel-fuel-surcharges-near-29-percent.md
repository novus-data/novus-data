<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>The US average retail price of on-highway diesel was $6.20 a gallon on 5 October 2026, against $3.71 a year earlier, according to <a href="https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">weekly EIA data</a>. It peaked at $6.53 on 21 September. Regular gasoline was $4.35, up $1.23 from a year earlier. The US Energy Information Administration said in its <a href="https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">6 October outlook</a> that diesel averaged $6.29 in September, and it expects $5.19 for 2026 as a whole and $4.49 in 2027.</p>
<p>Diesel has risen much faster than crude. The gap between New York Harbor ultra-low sulphur diesel and Brent crude averaged $93.51 a barrel in September 2026, against $32.37 in September 2025, according to Novus Data calculations from EIA daily spot prices for <a href="https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/hist/EER_EPD2DXL0_PF4_Y35NY_DPGD.htm" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">diesel</a> and <a href="https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/hist/RBRTED.htm" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Brent</a>. The International Energy Agency said in its September report that exports of refined products and liquefied petroleum gas from the Gulf were about 3.7 million barrels a day below February levels in August, <a href="https://www.worldoil.com/news/2026/9/11/iea-cuts-2026-oil-supply-outlook-as-gulf-recovery-slips-to-2027/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">World Oil reported</a>.</p>

<h2>Why it matters</h2>
<p>Parcel carriers pass diesel costs on to shippers through fuel surcharges that reset weekly. FedEx set its Ground fuel surcharge at 28.75% of the transport charge for the week of 12 to 18 October, down from 29.25% the week before, according to its <a href="https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/fuel-surcharge.html" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">published table</a>, which tracks the EIA's weekly diesel price with a lag. With diesel at or above $4.99, the FedEx Ground surcharge moves 0.25 percentage points for each 9-cent change in price, three times as steeply as below that level. <a href="https://www.ups.com/us/en/support/shipping-support/shipping-costs-rates/fuel-surcharges.page" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">UPS</a> publishes a similar diesel-linked schedule. Air services follow jet fuel instead: the FedEx surcharge on domestic package services other than Ground, which is based on the US Gulf Coast jet fuel price, rose to 32.25% for the week of 12 October from 31.75%, according to the same FedEx table.</p>
<p>Truckload prices show the same pressure. The producer price index for long-distance general freight truckload was 13.9% higher in August 2026 than a year earlier, according to Bureau of Labor Statistics data published by the <a href="https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/PCU484121484121" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">St Louis Fed</a>. The August consumer price index showed fuel oil, used for home heating in the US Northeast, up 52.0% on the year, according to the <a href="https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_09112026.htm" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">BLS</a>.</p>
<p>Because surcharges follow diesel with a lag, a fall in pump prices reaches freight bills only gradually. Diesel's 18-cent fall in the week to 5 October, from $6.38 to $6.20, moved the FedEx Ground surcharge down half a point, to 28.75%, for the week of 12 October, under FedEx's published table. The diesel premium over crude reflects lost refined-product exports as well as the price of crude, so freight costs could stay high even if crude falls (inferred).</p>

<p><em>Parcel shippers (sector).</em> Businesses shipping by FedEx Ground pay a fuel surcharge of 28.75% on transport charges in the week of 12 October. Confidence: reported. Source: FedEx fuel surcharge table, accessed 13 October 2026.</p>
<p><em>Truckload carriers and their customers (sector).</em> Long-distance truckload prices rose 13.9% year on year in August. Carriers pay for fuel before surcharges reimburse them, so smaller carriers with less cash carry the cost for longer. Confidence: reported for prices, inferred for the cash-flow effect. Source: BLS producer price index via the St Louis Fed.</p>

<h2>What would change this view</h2>
<ul>
<li><em>The diesel margin.</em> A sustained fall in the gap between diesel and Brent towards its September 2025 level of about $32 a barrel would show product supply recovering.</li>
<li><em>Gulf product exports.</em> A recovery in refined-product shipments from the Gulf would add supply to markets that have lost it.</li>
<li><em>The weekly EIA price.</em> It is published each week, and FedEx resets its surcharges weekly.</li>
</ul>
